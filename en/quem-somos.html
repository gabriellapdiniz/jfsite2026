History

A track record built on technique and trust

JF Aviation is a structural maintenance and repair company approved by ANAC under RBHA 145, with experienced and dedicated staff to perform high-quality aircraft maintenance.

In our hangar, inside Brasília International Airport, we have full technical capability and specialized support tooling to perform maintenance on aircraft such as the B727, B737, B767, B777, ERJ175, ATR42, ATR72, EMB120, DC10, A300, A318, A319, A320 and F100. We also keep a large stock of consumable parts to be more agile and efficient.

2002

Operations begin

In May 2002, JF Aviation began providing aircraft maintenance services.

2005

Own base

The company acquires its own operating base, expanding its service capacity.

2007

ANAC Standard C certification

Obtains ANAC Standard C certification, reinforcing its commitment to quality and safety.

2010

Technical expansion

Grows its technical staff and certifies the inclusion of new services under its addendum.

Today

Hangar in Brasília

We remain ANAC RBHA 145 certified, serving a wide commercial fleet from our hangar at Brasília International Airport.
